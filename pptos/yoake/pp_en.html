Privacy Policy

Coto System Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) handles information about users of the puzzle game Yoake (the “Service”) as described below.

1. Information stored on your device
Boards, scores, play records, daily records, settings, and similar information are stored on your device to resume play and display records. The Service does not offer account registration or cloud synchronization of game records. Device backups and restoration are subject to your operating system and settings.

2. Information handled by external services and its purposes
The Service uses the external services below. Even where we do not send names or similar details ourselves, SDK identifiers may distinguish use by the same device or installation.

• Firebase Analytics (Google): Screen views, aggregated interactions and play activity, device, operating system and app information, and SDK identifiers are used to analyze usage and improve the Service. We do not send entire boards, boards for each move, inquiry text, or email addresses as custom analytics events. Advertising ID collection by Analytics is disabled.
• Firebase Crashlytics (Google): Crash information, stack traces, device and app information, installation identifiers, and information about activity preceding a failure are used to investigate and resolve issues.
• Firebase Remote Config (Google): Firebase installation IDs and device and app information are used to retrieve settings such as the minimum supported version and ad display configuration.
• Google AdMob: When ad features are used, information such as approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic information is handled for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Advertising IDs are used on Android. On iOS, the Service does not request tracking permission or assume access to IDFA.
• Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Consent choices and their status are handled to obtain consent for ads and analytics in regions where required. Where a consent form is available, you may make choices as described in it. Where privacy options appear in the app settings, you may change your choices there.
• RevenueCat: App User IDs issued by the SDK, purchase history, receipts or purchase tokens, product and purchase status, and technical device and app information are handled to verify and restore the purchase that removes ads, prevent fraud, and analyze purchases. Purchases and payments are processed by Apple or Google through their stores. The Company does not obtain credit card numbers or store passwords.

The purchase that removes ads removes in-game ad displays. It does not stop analytics, crash diagnostics, configuration retrieval, or verification of purchase rights. Please also consult the providers’ official policies:
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3. Inquiries
Through email or an available in-app inquiry feature, we may receive inquiry text, an optional reply email address, and an image or video you select and attach. The in-app feature also sends information such as the operating system and app versions, language, and inquiry-related identifiers. We use this information to respond to inquiries and investigate issues.
Only media you select is attached; we do not obtain your entire photo library. However, we do not guarantee that all metadata is removed from selected files. Please avoid including personal information or information about others that is unnecessary for addressing your inquiry. You can contact us at info@cotosystem.com.

4. Processing by providers, third-party disclosure, and international processing
For the purposes above, we entrust processing to external service providers or send information to them. Information may be processed outside your country using the providers’ facilities. Their handling is governed by the official policies linked above. When the Company separately discloses personal information it holds to third parties, we follow the necessary consent or other lawful procedures, except where disclosure is permitted by law.

5. Retention and deletion
We retain inquiry information for as long as needed to address inquiries and delete information that is no longer needed. We respond to deletion requests in accordance with applicable law.
Information in external services is retained and deleted according to its purposes, provider policies, and our settings. Firebase Crashlytics begins deletion of crash information and associated identifiers from production and backup systems after 90 days. Retention of aggregated Analytics reports may differ from that of individual event and user information. Purchase rights and transaction records may be retained as needed to verify rights or meet legal obligations.
You can delete locally stored information through operating system options for deleting app data or uninstalling the app. This does not necessarily delete operating system backups, store transaction records, or information held by external services at the same time.

6. Security
We take security measures appropriate to the information handled, including access controls and protection of data in transit, to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, and similar incidents. We also review how external service providers handle information.

7. Your choices and requests
In regions where ad consent is required, you can make choices through available consent forms and settings. Android advertising IDs can be reset or deleted in operating system settings. Available options differ by operating system and region.
Contact us below regarding access, correction, suspension of use, deletion, or other statutory rights concerning personal information held by the Company. We verify identity only to the extent necessary and respond in accordance with applicable law. Where our ability to respond is limited, for example because an identifier cannot be identified, we explain the reason. For information such as transactions held by a store, you may need to contact the relevant provider.

8. Changes to this policy
When this policy changes, we publish the revised text and effective date on this page. For significant changes, we provide notice through the Service or another appropriate method according to the nature of the change, and obtain consent where required by law.
