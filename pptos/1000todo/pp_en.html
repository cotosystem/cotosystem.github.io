Privacy Policy

Information We Collect from Customers

We collect the following information from customers:

- Identification information generated through the use of cookies
- Information related to the customer’s operating system and device, such as OS-generated IDs, device type, and device identifiers
- Customer behavior history on our website, such as time spent on the site, input history, and purchase history
- Usage history of our application, such as launch times, input history, and purchase history

Purpose of Using Customer Information

We use the information collected from customers for the following purposes:

- To accept registrations for our services and to verify and authenticate customers
- To manage customers’ usage history of our services
- To process payment of usage fees
- To analyze customer behavior history on our services and improve and maintain our services
- To deliver, display, and measure the effectiveness of advertisements
- To display targeted advertisements tailored to customers’ preferences and interests
- To provide information regarding our services
- To send information about affiliated businesses and services
- To respond to customer inquiries
- To address acts that violate our terms or applicable laws
- To notify customers of changes, suspension, termination, or cancellation of our services
- To notify customers of changes to our terms and conditions
- In addition to the above, to provide, maintain, protect, and improve our services

Security Measures

If you would like information regarding the security measures we have taken for the management of information collected from customers, please contact us using the contact information provided at the end of this policy. We will respond individually in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Provision to Third Parties

Among the information collected from customers, we will not provide personal data (as defined in Article 16, Paragraph 3 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information) to third parties (including parties located outside Japan) without obtaining the customer’s prior consent, except in the following cases:

- When outsourcing the handling of personal data to external parties
- When our company or our services are acquired
- When jointly using the information with business partners (details of such joint use will be separately announced where applicable)
- When otherwise permitted by law

Customers can refuse the collection of this information by disabling cookies in their browser settings. Please check your browser settings for more details. https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/jp/
