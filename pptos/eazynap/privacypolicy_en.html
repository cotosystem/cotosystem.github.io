Privacy Policy

Coto System Co., Ltd. (the "Company") handles user information in the sleep support app "eazynap" (the "Service") as described below.

1. Information stored on your device
Settings such as voice, ambient sound, volume, speech, and timer; scenes and favorites (including the names you give them); reminder settings and times; records of onboarding and review requests; the result of the last check of your Pro status; and downloaded audio data are stored on your device to provide the Service. The Service has no account registration and no feature that syncs this information to the cloud. Whether this information is included in device backups and restores depends on the OS and your settings.

2. Information handled by external services, and purposes
The Service uses the following external services. Even where the Company does not itself send your name, email address, or similar information, the identifiers used by these services may make it possible to distinguish use on the same device or installation.

• Firebase Analytics (Google): Screen views, the start and end of sleep sessions, use of features such as the timer, voices, and ambient sounds, paywall views and purchase results, device, OS, and app information, and identifiers such as the app instance ID (including the advertising ID, to the extent permitted by OS settings and permissions) are used to analyze usage and improve the Service. Favorite names and reminder times are not sent. In regions where consent is required, collection follows your consent choices.
• Firebase Crashlytics (Google): Crash and error information (stack traces and the state of the app at the time), device, OS, and app information, session information, installation identifiers, and similar information are used to investigate and fix problems.
• Google AdMob: To show ads (see "3. Advertising"), the approximate location estimated from your IP address, device identifiers (including the advertising ID), ad views and interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and similar information are handled for ad delivery, ad measurement, and fraud prevention. Google and its partners may be involved in delivering and measuring ads.
• Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Determines whether consent is required and, in regions where it is, asks for your consent and choices regarding ads and measurement and stores the result on your device. On iOS, UMP also shows the explanation before you are asked for permission to track. To make this determination and show these screens, Google processes device and app information, your IP address, and similar information.
• RevenueCat: The anonymous user ID issued by the SDK (App User ID), purchase history, receipts or purchase tokens, product and subscription status, store country and currency, technical device and app information, and similar information are handled to verify and restore Pro access, prevent fraud, and analyze purchases. Purchases and payments are made through the Apple or Google store, and the Company does not obtain your credit card number or store password.
• GitHub Pages (GitHub): Delivers voice audio data. When audio data is downloaded, connection information such as your IP address is processed by the hosting provider.

3. Advertising
On the free plan (including after a free trial or subscription has ended), the Service shows Google AdMob native ads on the home screen, the screen for choosing voices and ambient sounds, and the settings screen. Ads are not shown while a sleep session is playing or paused. Ads are not shown with Pro (during a free trial or subscription).
The ad SDK is initialized and ads are loaded only after the required consent has been checked. Consent checks and initialization of the ad SDK may take place regardless of your plan.
The maximum content rating for ads is set to General audiences (G).
Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent choices, OS settings, and regional regulations.

4. Advertising identifiers and consent management
• In regions where consent or choices are required, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and some U.S. states, the UMP screen asks for your consent and choices regarding ads and measurement.
• On iOS, an explanation is shown before you are asked for permission to track. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for ad delivery and measurement only if you allow tracking. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
• On Android, the advertising ID is used for ad delivery and measurement. Firebase Analytics also collects the advertising ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings (the location of the setting varies by device and OS version).
• You can change your consent and choices at any time in the Service under Settings > Support > Ad privacy settings. This item appears only in regions where such choices are required.

5. Notifications
Reminders (Pro) and the notice before a free trial ends are local notifications scheduled on your device. Notification times are not sent to the Company or to external services. Notifications are shown only if you allow them in the OS settings.

6. Purposes of use
The Company uses the information it obtains to provide, maintain, and improve the Service; analyze usage; investigate problems; verify and restore Pro access; deliver and measure ads; prevent fraud; respond to inquiries; address conduct that violates laws or our terms; and notify you of changes to the Service or this Policy.

7. Outsourcing, provision to third parties, and processing outside Japan
For the purposes above, the Company entrusts the processing of information to, or sends information to, the providers of the external services. Information may be processed outside Japan (for example, in the United States) on the providers' facilities. Providers handle information in accordance with their official policies listed above. If the Company provides personal information that it separately holds to a third party, it will follow the required consent or other lawful procedures, except in cases such as outsourcing, business succession, or where permitted by law.

8. Retention and deletion
Information held by external services is retained and deleted according to its purpose, the provider's policies, and the Company's settings. In Firebase Crashlytics, deletion of crash information and associated identifiers from production and backup systems begins after 90 days. Records of purchase entitlements and transactions may be retained for as long as necessary to verify entitlements or to comply with legal obligations.
Information on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. However, OS backups, store transaction records, and information already sent to external services are not necessarily deleted at the same time.

9. Security
To prevent unauthorized access, leakage, loss, and similar incidents, the Company takes security measures appropriate to the information handled, such as access control and protection during transmission. For details of the measures taken, please contact us at the address at the end of this Policy, and we will respond individually in accordance with applicable law.

10. Your requests and contact
If you wish to request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, deletion, or similar actions regarding personal information held by the Company, please contact us at the address at the end of this Policy. We will verify your identity to the extent necessary and respond in accordance with applicable law. If we cannot identify the relevant identifiers or our ability to respond is otherwise limited, we will explain why. For transaction information held by the stores and similar information, you may need to follow each provider's procedures.

11. Changes to this Policy
If we change this Policy, we will post the revised content and its effective date on this page and notify you of important changes through appropriate means, such as within the Service.
