Privacy Policy

Coto System Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) describes below how information is handled in Ekizen (易禅, the “Service”).

1. Records stored on your device
Questions, readings, answers, small actions, reflections, unfinished readings, and the collection of encountered hexagrams are stored on your device. The Service has no feature that automatically sends this text to our servers or analytics services, no user account registration, and no cloud sync for your records. Operating-system backups depend on your device settings and the backup provider.

2. Information submitted with feedback
When you press Send, we transmit the feedback type and message, an optional reply email address, one image or video you select, display language, app and OS versions, platform, a feedback installation identifier, and a submission identifier. Questions and journal entries are not attached automatically. Information you include in the message or attachment will be transmitted. Please avoid unnecessary personal information and information about others. We do not guarantee that all information within an attachment is removed.
We use this information to respond, investigate issues, resolve problems, and suppress duplicate submissions. It passes through our feedback infrastructure on Google Cloud to a Discord support destination managed by the Company. Infrastructure providers may process connection information associated with the transmission.

3. Usage measurement and configuration delivery
Production iOS and Android apps use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand usage and improve the Service. In addition to fixed screen names, the SDK processes app launch and usage information, device and app information, and identifiers such as app instance identifiers. We do not pass question, answer, or reflection text as analytics parameters. We do not collect your local writing as an “anonymous input history.”
Analytics advertising personalization is disabled by default. This does not disable all measurement or processing of all identifiers. App collection is disabled for development and preview use.
We use Firebase Remote Config to obtain settings such as the minimum supported build. Google processes app and installation information needed to deliver those settings.

4. Purchase and advertising information
As of October 28, 2026, paid product sales configuration and advertising delivery are being prepared. Advertising delivery is currently disabled.
If paid features are offered, the Apple or Google store and RevenueCat process transaction information, receipts or purchase tokens, the SDK's app user identifier, and device and app information needed for purchases, restoration, and access verification. Our app does not ask for or collect card numbers. Feedback reply addresses are not automatically registered with RevenueCat.
If advertising is enabled, the Service uses Google AdMob and UMP. The design initializes the advertising SDK after required consent checks and displays a banner in the hexagram library for users whose free access status has been confirmed. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device and other identifiers, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, and performance information. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not eliminate processing needed for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement. Where ad privacy options are required, they can be accessed in the app's settings. We will recheck configuration and this policy before starting these services.

5. External services and recipients
Google (Firebase, Google Cloud, and AdMob), Discord, and, for purchases, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process information for the purposes described above. Information may be processed outside Japan depending on the providers' locations and processing environments. See also their information:
Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Discord: https://discord.com/privacy
RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
When you open source references, store pages, or other external links, the destination's policies apply.

6. Purposes and disclosure to third parties
We use information to provide features, respond to feedback, verify access, address failures and misuse, and maintain and improve the Service. We do not disclose personal information to third parties without the individual's consent except for processing entrusted to providers necessary for these services, transmissions initiated by the user, cases required or permitted by law, and similar applicable exceptions.

7. Retention and deletion
You can delete local journal entries from the journal screen. Deleting an entry does not remove primary hexagrams from your encountered collection. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove your local records. Backups are managed through the operating system.
Feedback messages, reply addresses, and attachments are retained for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and deleted when no longer needed. There is no automatic deletion after a fixed number of days. Deleting journal entries or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete submitted feedback. Contact us below to request deletion.
Information handled by SDKs is also subject to the providers' settings and policies. Deleting local records does not automatically delete previously transmitted analytics data or transaction records. We respond to requests after necessary verification and with regard to legal retention obligations and other applicable requirements.

8. Security
Feedback is transmitted over HTTPS. We manage access to information we handle and work to maintain appropriate safeguards. We respond to inquiries about specific security measures in accordance with applicable law.

9. Requests and contact
For access, correction, suspension of use, deletion, or related requests, contact info@cotosystem.com. Please provide information needed to locate the data, such as the app name and approximate feedback submission date. We perform identification and verification to the extent necessary. We cannot retrieve or restore records that exist only on your device.

10. Changes to this policy
When our information handling changes, we will revise this policy as necessary and communicate the effective date and changes through appropriate means.
